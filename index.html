Client Guide

Your Healing Journey & After-Care

Proper aftercare represents 50% of your permanent makeup success. Follow this essential timeline for crisp healed hair strokes and rich, even color retention.

Day 1 • Procedure Day

Initial Care

30 minutes post-treatment, gently wipe the area with your provided cotton pad in soft circular motions. Pat dry and apply a very thin layer of aftercare cream. Repeat every hour until bedtime.

Days 2 to 7

Daily Maintenance

Every morning and evening, wipe gently with a damp cotton pad, pat completely dry, and apply a thin layer of aftercare cream. If the skin feels tight, repeat lightly. Never pick scabs!

Days 5 to 12

The Milky Phase

As delicate flaking finishes, the strokes may temporarily look lighter or milky. This is new healing skin layer and completely normal. The pigment will bloom back in week 2–3!

Days 12 to 21+

Color Rebound

The true rich undertone surfaces as the deeper layer heals. At 4 weeks, return for your complimentary perfection touch-up to lock in density for the next 1–2 years!

What to Avoid for 30 Days
  • ✕ Direct sunbathing, tanning beds, and UV solarium lamps.
  • ✕ Chemical peels, fruit acids (AHA/BHA), and microdermabrasion.
  • ✕ Active regenerative creams containing Retin-A or Glycolic acid.
  • ✕ Laser treatments over or near the treated areas.
  • ✕ Heavy sweat workouts, saunas, and swimming pools during the first 10 days.
Long-Term Maintenance Tips
  • ✓ Daily SPF 30+ sunscreen over healed brows to keep colors fresh.
  • ✓ Schedule your 9–12 month maintenance touch-up to sustain sharpness.
  • ✓ Keep chemical exfoliants 1 inch away from your brow/liner boundaries.
  • ✓ Stay hydrated to maintain plump skin texture.
